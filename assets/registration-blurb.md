# Registration blurb (the first participant experience — M1)

## Short version (event listing)

**The Meridian Challenge — catch the wrong answer.**

An AI assistant will answer business questions about a real company's data.
Some of its answers will be confidently, silently wrong. In 2.5 hours and in
pairs, you'll catch one yourself — and learn the fixes that actually work.
No programming needed: if you can paste text and read a number, you're
qualified.

You leave with:

1. **A caught lie** — a wrong AI answer you personally diagnosed.
2. **The 10-question checklist** for auditing any AI-generated data answer.
3. **A rerun-at-home kit** — the data, the prompts, and a one-page recipe
   you can run against your own company's warehouse.

Seats are in pairs; come with a colleague or we'll pair you.

## Longer version (internal comms / agenda page)

- 0:00 Doors, setup pre-done — you log in and go.
- 0:20 The experiment: five business questions, raw data. Score yourself.
- 0:55 The fix: your team declares the business rules; the assistant
  rebuilds the metrics. Same questions, new score.
- 1:50 The hunt: teams trade questions designed to break the assistant.
- 2:15 The debrief: why enterprise accuracy collapses (public benchmarks:
  ~90% lab, ~20% real) and what to steal for work on Monday.

Prereqs: a laptop with a browser; curiosity. Everything else is provided.
